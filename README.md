### Alberto Aragón Jiménez

Final-year Computer Engineering student at the University of Granada, specialising in Computing and Intelligent Systems. I like working through the maths behind an algorithm, implementing it and figuring out why it produces the results it does, mostly in machine learning and optimisation.

**Selected work**

- [woa-cec2017](https://github.com/Albertoaj05/woa-cec2017): Whale Optimization Algorithm and two memetic hybrids in C++, benchmarked on CEC2017 at 10, 30 and 50 dimensions. The best hybrid beats plain WOA on 30/30 functions at D = 50; every run is reproducible.
- [portfolio-metaheuristics](https://github.com/Albertoaj05/portfolio-metaheuristics): 14 metaheuristics (genetic, memetic, GRASP, ILS, simulated annealing) for risk-adjusted portfolio selection on the IBEX 35, S&P 100 and S&P 500; fitted on 2015–2024 and tested on 2025, with Wilcoxon tests over 50 runs.
- [machine-learning-coursework](https://github.com/Albertoaj05/machine-learning-coursework): three notebooks from my Machine Learning course: classification and regression with scikit-learn and XGBoost (96.75 % accuracy), clustering and association rules, and deep learning with Keras.
- [spanish-seq2seq-chatbot](https://github.com/Albertoaj05/spanish-seq2seq-chatbot): a generative chatbot in Spanish with a Seq2Seq LSTM in Keras. Pretrained word embeddings raise accuracy on unseen phrasings from 80 % to 96 %, compared over five seeds with attention and a retrieval baseline.
- [search-agents-java](https://github.com/Albertoaj05/search-agents-java): depth-first, A*, RTA* and LRTA*(k) agents in Java for a grid puzzle game, compared on route length, expanded nodes and time. A* always finds the optimal route but expands up to ten times more nodes.
- [parchis-adversarial-search](https://github.com/Albertoaj05/parchis-adversarial-search): an AI player for Parchís in C++, with Minimax, alpha-beta pruning and four heuristics of my own. The final version, alpha-beta with dynamic depth, beats all three reference bots playing from either side.
- [quantum-playground](https://github.com/Albertoaj05/quantum-playground): eight quantum algorithms in Qiskit, including a VQE that reaches the H2 ground-state energy within 10⁻⁵ Ha. [Results page](https://albertoaj05.github.io/quantum-playground/).
